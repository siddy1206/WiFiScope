Academic Foundation, Mathematical Specifications & Real References

Current Prototype (Validated)

  • Spatial 2D Least-Squares signal surface regression.
  • Single-floor dead-zone identification with persistent thresholding.
  • Model-based router placement coordinate recommendation.
  • Real-time interactive canvas visualization & contour mapping.
  • Deterministic held-out validation ($MAE = 1.19\text{ dBm}, R^2 = 0.941$).

Future Roadmap (Scalable Vision)

  • Multi-AP Mesh Optimization: Joint spatial optimization for enterprise mesh routers.
  • Architectural CAD / Floor-Plan Overlay: Upload PNG/DWG floorplans.
  • Multi-Floor 3D Modeling: Elevation attenuation across building levels.
  • Gaussian Process Uncertainty: Explicit predictive variance modeling.
